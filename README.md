# 🍡 My Favorite Anime: *Gintama*

## Why I Love It

- **Genre**: Comedy, Action, Sci-fi, Parody  
- **Characters**: Especially the Odd Jobs trio — Gintoki, Kagura, and Shinpachi — who are chaotic, lovable, and surprisingly heroic

> *"I don't care if this country of Samurai fell. Then and now, what I protect has never changed!"* — Sakata Gintoki

## Top 3 Arcs

1. **Shinsengumi Crisis Arc**
2. **Benizakura Arc**
3. **Yagyuu Arc**

## Additional Thoughts

Gintama is a wildly humorous anime that thrives on parody and absurdity, but beneath the chaos lies a powerful theme of *found family*.  
The Odd Jobs crew may seem unserious and unhinged, but they always find ways to help others and protect what matters. The sudden shift into serious arcs can be a bit of a whiplash — but it’s part of what makes the series so emotionally resonant.

Found family  
: A group of people who form deep, familial bonds despite not being related by blood. These relationships are built on trust, loyalty, and shared experiences—often stronger than traditional family ties.
